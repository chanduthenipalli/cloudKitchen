OUR KITCHEN

One pot. One seal. No shortcuts — only time.

Deccan Handi runs a tight, focused kitchen — five biryanis, two sides, done properly, instead of fifty dishes done in a hurry. Every handi is sealed with dough and cooked on dum, so the rice finishes in the meat's own steam, not a shortcut sauce.
